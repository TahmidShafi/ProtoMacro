Step 07

Track quitting or reducing habits with streaks, setbacks and trigger analytics. A setback is data, not failure — your progress still matters.

Reset is a self-monitoring tool, not medical treatment or professional advice. If a habit is harming your health, please consider speaking with a qualified professional.
